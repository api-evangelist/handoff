## Key Differentiators

- **AI-native from day one** — not a legacy tool with AI added later
- **Automated takeoffs connected to your estimate** — processes your plan set in the background and delivers a color-coded PDF and a written summary flagging assumptions, missing information, and plan clashes.
- **Full job lifecycle** — CRM → takeoff → estimate → proposal → scheduling → project management → change orders → invoicing → payment, all in one platform
- **Localized pricing** — estimates use local labor rates and material costs, not national averages
- **Mobile-first** — built for the job site; proposals can be sent from a client's driveway before you leave
- **Speed** — users report going from hours to seconds on initial estimates
- **File intelligence** — photos, drawings, and documents generate estimates without manual input

## Competitive Context

Handoff competes in the residential construction management and estimating software category. Comparable tools include:

- **Buildertrend** — broad project management platform for home builders; strong on scheduling and client portals, but estimating and takeoffs stay manual and it's not AI-native
- **JobTread** — job costing and project management, geared toward commercial and home builders; solid financials, but produces estimates manually and isn't residential-first
- **Jobber** — field service management with a basic AI co-pilot; good for scheduling and dispatching, but not AI-native and not built around residential estimating
- **Housecall Pro** — home services CRM and scheduling for recurring service businesses; not built around construction estimating, no AI-powered or localized pricing
- **Buildxact** — estimating and project management with takeoff tools for residential contractors; AI is a paid add-on rather than built-in
- **Houzz Pro** — full-suite platform for remodelers with CRM, 3D floor plans, and selections; broader scope, but AI features are limited and it can be complex/expensive for teams that don't need mood boards and 3D planning
- **Contractor Foreman** — broad construction management at a lower price point; less AI-native, more manual workflows
- **Joist** — invoicing and estimating app for single-trade contractors and handymen; simple and mobile-first, but limited to single-trade jobs with no AI estimating or real-time local pricing
- **Bluebeam** — PDF markup and takeoff tool used widely in commercial AEC; strong for drawing annotation and collaboration, but lacks estimating, invoicing, or project management

Handoff's differentiation is its AI-first approach to speed and accuracy across the full job lifecycle — from the first estimate through final invoice — built specifically for residential scopes with a vast construction knowledge base (1build).
